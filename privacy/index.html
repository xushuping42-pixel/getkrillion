Privacy and browser storage

What this independent guide stores and measures.

Browser preferences

Reviewed prompts, expanded hints and checklist selections are stored in your browser using localStorage. Clearing site data removes those preferences. This guide does not require an account or store an official game score.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits and use of hints, copying and search tools. Analytics sends usage information to Google and can use cookies or browser identifiers. Our custom events do not include typed search terms or copied answer text. This site does not claim that nothing leaves your browser.

Answer data and external links

Answer records originate from the public official reveal. Opening the official game or a reference link takes you to another site's privacy practices. Searching the saved records runs in your browser after the dated data is downloaded.
