Krillion Roblox: Where to Play & How It Works

There is a Krillion listing on Roblox. Check the creator and platform before joining friends or following advice written for the browser game.

View Krillion on Roblox ↗

Sources reviewed November 24, 2026 · External games may change.

The short answer

The Roblox listing names Hypr Games as its creator. It is a separate destination from krillion.io; matching names do not establish shared accounts, rooms or answer data.

Where is Krillion on Roblox?

Open the Roblox Krillion listing and check the creator, Hypr Games. Its title included an Unlimited label when reviewed. That label does not establish access to the Unlimited mode on krillion.io.

Compare the address with your friend's invitation. If your group plans to play on Roblox, an unrelated browser-room invitation will not identify the same session.

What gameplay does the listing describe?

The description presents rare-answer questions and advertises parties with friends, public lobbies and private games using a party code. It also mentions voice-chat support. These are claims from the listing, not features we verified by completing a live match.

Check the current page and game screens for availability and requirements. A store page alone does not establish current server activity. We cannot promise that voice chat or a particular lobby will be available to every visitor.

Will this site's answers work on Roblox?

We cannot promise that. Our records come from dated krillion.io reveals and do not establish which answers a Roblox experience accepts. Use a record only when the provider, edition and complete prompt match.
