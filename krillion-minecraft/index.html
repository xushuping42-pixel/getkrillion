Krillion Minecraft: What Is Minellion?

“Minecraft Krillion” can refer to a separate Minecraft-themed trivia game. Minellion is the name to check for the daily game made by Hydro.

Visit Minellion ↗

Sources reviewed November 24, 2026 · External games may change.

The short answer

Minellion describes itself as a Minecraft game in the style of Krillion. It is a separate fan project, not an official Minecraft product or a Krillion themed pack.

What is the Minecraft game called?

Minellion is Hydro's browser-based Minecraft trivia game. Its FAQ acknowledges that players call it Minecraft Krillion. Keep its name and address separate from krillion.io when following a video, joining friends or looking for instructions.

The official Krillion packs page lists Words & Language, Movies, Sports and Geography, plus forthcoming Music. It does not list a Minecraft pack at review time. A general prompt involving Minecraft would not, by itself, establish that such a pack exists.

How does Minellion's daily game work?

Its daily dig has seven Minecraft prompts. Less commonly chosen answers dig deeper, with bedrock as the goal. Its provider says the daily set changes at 00:00 UTC. Keep that schedule separate from the Krillion edition dates on this site.

Before comparing a result with somebody else's screenshot, check both the game and edition. Similar rare-answer mechanics do not imply shared answer lists.

How to approach a Minecraft prompt

Identify the kind of thing requested: a mob, item, block or biome. Then read every restriction before selecting something unusual. An obscure name still needs to fit the category.

If a guess is not counted, use that game's own instructions to check naming and grouping. Do not assume colour or material variants always count separately. Our dated Krillion answer finder is not a Minellion answer database.

Can I play it with friends?

Minellion has a separate mode called Creeper Party. Find it through Minellion's navigation, rather than following a Krillion Puffer Party invitation. For the original game's party destination, use our multiplayer guide.

Do I need a Minecraft download?

The provider describes Minellion as browser-based and says it has no app. If another destination asks you to install a mod, verify what product you are opening rather than assuming it is the same trivia game.
