What is krillion unlimited?

krillion unlimited is a free daily guessing game. You get seven prompts, and for each one you name as many valid answers as you can before you run out of tries. Your best answer on each prompt counts. Answers score max(5, 100 − rank), so rank 1 earns 99 points. Your seven scores are shown against a 700-point scale. No download, no sign-up — the game starts the moment the page opens.

How to play

Read the prompt, type an answer, and press Enter. You have three tries per prompt. Answers are matched against a ranked list, so the more common your answer, the higher it scores. When all seven prompts are done, the round ends and you can copy your score card and share it.

Daily round vs unlimited mode

The daily round changes at midnight Eastern Time. Unlimited mode lets you keep playing new rounds from the same question bank, so you can practise without waiting a day. Your best score and streak are stored in your own browser — nothing is sent to a server.

Seven categories

Every round draws from the same seven categories: Animals, Geography, Food, Movies & TV, Music, Sports and Trivia. The mix is shuffled each round, so no two games feel the same.
