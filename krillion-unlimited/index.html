The short answer

The official menu has an Unlimited entry. This independent guide explains where to go; it does not run an unlimited game or unlock paid content.

Where to play Krillion Unlimited

Start with the Unlimited entry on krillion.io. The game itself, any unlock screen and your game progress remain with its provider. Before buying anything, read the access conditions displayed there. This guide does not promise a particular price, free access or an endless supply of fresh questions.

If you want answers rather than another game, use our recorded answer archive. Reading a past reveal is a reference activity, rather than a new attempt in the official game.

Practise without spoiling the next round

  1. Decide whether you want a fresh game or an explanation of a finished one.
  2. For a finished edition, compare your reasoning with a dated answer list.
  3. Read the full prompt conditions before borrowing an answer from another round.
  4. Use a small hint first; reveal an example only when you want one.

Our progressive hints and rarity guide help you review a recorded round. An answer or score from one prompt does not establish what another mode accepts.

Does Unlimited mean multiplayer?

Do not use those names interchangeably. To play with friends, start with our Krillion multiplayer guide and the separate official Puffer Party entry.
