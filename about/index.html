About Krillion Fieldnotes

A dated answer record, a useful hint, and a clear source.

Independent from the official game

Krillion Fieldnotes at getkrillion.xyz is an independent answer and hints guide. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by krillion.io. Play the game on the official Krillion website.

Where the answers come from

Prompts, answer spellings, aliases, tiers and scores come from the date-matched official published reveal. We preserve a complete seven-prompt snapshot. We omit answer quips and official artwork. Original short editorial notes identify their own reference links.

How dates and updates work

The daily date follows America/New_York. Automated checks run hourly and determine the current date in America/New_York, including daylight saving changes. A valid saved record avoids another request for that date. Checks and publishing can be delayed. Only a complete validated reveal can replace an edition; if a new one is unavailable, the previous record keeps its actual date.

What a hint or search result means

First-letter and word-shape hints describe one recorded example, not every answer. Search includes aliases from the selected saved date. Missing dates remain missing, and a missing answer here does not prove the live game rejects it. For a correction, compare the linked official source and current game.
