How scoring works

Every accepted answer has a popularity rank. Rank 1 is the most common answer and is worth 100 points; each rank below it is worth a little less, with a floor of 5 points. A prompt is capped at 100 points and a full round at 700.

krillion hints vs full answers

The daily pages give both. If you only want a nudge, read the first few entries of a prompt's list — the top answers are the ones most players name first. If you want the whole list, keep reading; nothing is hidden.

Past rounds

Rounds are archived by date and never rewritten, so an old page always shows the prompts that were live that day. Bookmark a date if you want to compare a later round against it.
