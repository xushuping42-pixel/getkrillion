Rarity has context.

The official FAQ describes question-dependent methods: some use familiarity data, while others involve model assistance and manual review. The special Krillion pick is not chosen purely by rarity. A word sounding obscure to you does not establish its tier.

Our guide preserves a returned score. It does not calculate a new official rarity score from an answer’s position in a list or the frequency of searches on this site.
